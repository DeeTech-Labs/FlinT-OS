# FlinT commercial licensing

5 November 2026. Translation of the Russian original.

FlinT OS, Flint Flare and Ignite Core/Runtime use PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 to the extent of DeeTech Labs' rights. OS and Core/Runtime also receive the separate [community permission](COMMUNITY-EXCEPTION.md). This is source-available; a noncommercial restriction does not meet the OSI open source definition.

Commercial uses beyond existing permissions require a separate written agreement with the relevant rights holders: device manufacturing, profitable sales, OEM integration, commercial embedding or distribution of platform code. This document is not a commercial license, sets no price and promises no approval. Contact maintainers through [DeeTech Labs](https://github.com/DeeTech-Labs), specifying components, versions, distribution and intended use.

## Independent Ignite applications and SDK

Authors retain their own application code. Public headers/APIs, specifications, examples and separable SDK tools use Apache-2.0 as mapped in IgniteSDK's LICENSE. Independent free and paid Ignite applications are allowed without assigning copyright to DeeTech Labs. This does not authorize embedding Runtime into a third-party commercial product. Runtime-linked development tools receive the separate permission in SDK-DEVELOPMENT-PERMISSION.md. A future marketplace may impose separate terms; this document does not create one.

## Earlier versions and third-party rights

New terms do not revoke Apache-2.0 or other rights in previously received releases. Third-party notices and dependency licenses remain intact. DeeTech Labs grants only rights it owns; consent from other authors is not presumed. Contributions neither assign copyright nor grant unrestricted commercial relicensing rights. Commercial relicensing of others' contributions needs their separate consent.

FlinT Web code stays Apache-2.0: the shared discussion selected no replacement for the website. New standalone licensing documentation uses Apache-2.0 except for operative legal permissions and unmodified license texts. Technical documentation follows its component's license. Language rules do not change licenses.

## Hardware and brand

Schematics, PCB, Gerber, CAD and enclosures need explicit license designations. The [hardware draft](HARDWARE-LICENSE.md) does not automatically apply to files, third-party boards or the idea of an ESP32-S3 clock. Pinout documentation does not imply ownership of a third-party board. The [brand policy](TRADEMARKS.md) distinguishes compatibility from official origin.
