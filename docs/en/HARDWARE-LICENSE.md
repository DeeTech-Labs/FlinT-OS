# FlinT Hardware Community License — draft 1.0

Draft, 5 November 2026. Translation of the Russian original. Not an effective license for any hardware files in these repositories. Adoption requires an explicit file and rights-holder list.

The target model for DeeTech Labs' own hardware designs permits study, modification and noncommercial redistribution of schematics, PCB, Gerber, CAD and enclosure models, and fabrication for personal, research, educational and hobby use. Hobby transfers to natural persons should allow documented component, material, fabrication, procurement-duty and shipping costs, excluding profit, the assembler's labor, royalties, organized serial production and commercial sales channels. Notices and unofficial-build labeling must be retained.

Commercial fabrication using licensed designs should require a separate written agreement. No brand rights, ownership of third-party boards or components, functional ideas or independent implementations is granted or claimed. Mandatory safety and legal requirements remain; no additional warranties are intended.

Existing file licenses remain applicable until a final text and explicit scope are adopted. This draft does not relicense pinouts, ESP32-S3 Super Mini or ST7789 or claim them as DeeTech Labs designs.
